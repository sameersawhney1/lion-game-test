# The Weight of Struggle

In the quiet hours before the dawn,
When doubt creeps in like morning fog,
We find ourselves pressed down and worn,
Each step a battle through the bog.

The struggle is not always loud,
Sometimes it whispers in the night,
A voice that speaks within the crowd,
Of battles lost, of fading light.

Yet in this weight, we find our strength,
The scars become our greatest proof,
That we have walked the hardest length,
And found our truth beneath the roof.

For struggle shapes the human soul,
It teaches us what we can bear,
It makes the broken pieces whole,
And teaches us to truly care.

So when the burden feels too great,
And mountains tower to the sky,
Remember: it's not sealed by fate,
The struggle is how we learn to fly.

In every fall, there is a rise,
In every tear, a seed of grace,
The struggle is the greatest prize—
It's what defines the human race.
