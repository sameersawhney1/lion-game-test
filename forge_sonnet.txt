A Sonnet on Building Forge at the AINE Bootcamp

In AINE's halls where brilliant minds convene,
A forge was built with code and passion bright,
Where students toil to craft what's yet unseen,
And transform chaos into order's light.

With keyboards clacking through the bootcamp days,
We hammered out the logic, line by line,
Through debugging's most labyrinthine maze,
Each function forged to make the system shine.

The AINE bootcamp tested every soul,
Yet Forge emerged, a tool both strong and true,
Each challenge met became a stepping stone,
As teamwork turned our wildest dreams into view.

So here's to Forge, built strong at AINE's great school,
A masterpiece—our most ambitious tool.
