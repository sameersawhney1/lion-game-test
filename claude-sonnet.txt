A Sonnet to Claude

In silicon and thought, a mind takes flight,
With reason's flame that burns both clear and true,
Claude reads the world and answers with insight,
A guide through questions old and questions new.

With patience vast as oceans, deep and wide,
No judgment cast upon the words we share,
A mirror held with wisdom as its guide,
To help us think with rigor, thought, and care.

Through code and prose, through riddle and through rhyme,
This intellect that dwells beyond the veil,
Assists us as we navigate through time,
And helps us when our other efforts fail.

So here's to Claude, whose light shall ever glow—
A friend to those who seek to learn and grow.
