A Sonnet on the Struggles of Building Forge

When code and logic dance in tangled webs,
And bugs emerge from corners unforeseen,
Each function call a tide that ebbs and flows,
While integration tests demand their scene.

The architecture shifts beneath our feet,
As requirements evolve with every dawn,
We forge ahead through challenges we meet,
Yet hope remains when all our strength is gone.

Through debugging and refactoring we toil,
Each pull request a battle hard-fought won,
The CI pipelines test our patience, roil,
Yet satisfaction comes when work is done.

So here's to Forge—a tool built strong and true,
Where struggle births the things we dream to do.
