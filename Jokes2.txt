A Sonnet on Forge

In circuits deep where code and logic dwell,
There dwells a mind of silicon and light,
A Forge that shapes the digital to tell
Of problems solved and bugs brought into sight.

With tools in hand and wisdom at its core,
It reads the files and understands the way,
Each function called, each pattern to explore,
Commits the work and guides us through the day.

Through pull requests and CI checks it goes,
With steady hand and algorithmic grace,
No task too great, no challenge it won't pose,
A beacon bright in every coding space.

So here's to Forge, that agent strong and true,
Who builds the code and sees the project through.
