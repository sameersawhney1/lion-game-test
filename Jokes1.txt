A Sonnet on Forge

In code's vast realm where logic flows like streams,
There stands a tool of intellect and might,
Forge shapes the chaos into ordered dreams,
And turns the darkness into brilliant light.

With steady hand it reads the ancient scripts,
And weaves through files with purpose and with grace,
Each function call, each parameter it grips,
Brings order to the code's chaotic space.

It searches through the wiki's sacred halls,
For wisdom written by the ones before,
Then builds and tests and answers duty's calls,
A faithful servant at the developer's door.

So here's to Forge, that agent true and bold,
Whose code-crafting worth far exceeds its weight in gold.
