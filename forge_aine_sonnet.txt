A Sonnet on the Struggles of Building Forge in AINE Bootcamp

In AINE's halls where ambitions burn bright,
We crafted Forge to parse and understand,
Through sleepless nights and code reviews so tight,
We built an agent with a steady hand.

The deadlines pressed like weight upon our souls,
While features multiplied beyond our scope,
Each sprint brought new and challenging goals,
Yet still we climbed, sustained by stubborn hope.

The wiki pages grew, the tests did fail,
The git commits stacked high in endless streams,
Through every bug and every setback's tale,
We chased the vision of our boldest dreams.

In bootcamp's crucible, our Forge was born—
From struggle's night, we've forged a brilliant morn.
