A Sonnet on the Struggles of Building Forge

When code and logic dance in tangled webs,
And bugs emerge from shadows unforeseen,
The builder's will and confidence both ebbs,
As errors mock what once was clean and keen.

The integration tests that fail at night,
The merge conflicts that twist the developer's mind,
The endless loops of debugging the plight,
Where solutions hide and are so hard to find.

Yet still we forge ahead through every trial,
Each failure teaches lessons yet untold,
We build with grit, with patience, and with style,
Creating tools more precious far than gold.

For in the struggle lies the builder's art—
Each line of code, a piece of the heart.
