A Poem on AINE Forge
====================

In circuits deep where logic hums and flows,
A forge was built where sharp ideas grow.
Not iron struck on anvil, hot and bright,
But code and thought refined through careful light.

AINE Forge — where agents take their form,
Where plans are laid before the work is born.
No step is skipped, no shortcut blindly made,
Each branch a craft, each commit cleanly laid.

It reads before it writes, it thinks before it acts,
It cites the file, it sticks to only facts.
No filler words, no emojis in the way,
Just clean, direct, professional display.

So here it stands, this forge of modern mind,
Where human will and agent skill are twined.
A tool that builds with purpose, care, and art —
AINE Forge: where every repo gets a start.
